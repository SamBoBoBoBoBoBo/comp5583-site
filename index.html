01
11/2020–03/2021

Research on Life Insurance Customer Classification Based on Data Mining Techniques

Supervisor: Yao Jiayi

Purpose

Customer classification for a life insurance company, providing data support and a decision-making basis for customer relationship management and targeted marketing.

View my contribution

My contribution

  • Assisted the advisor in completing the customer classification section of a research project for a life insurance company.
  • Utilized SQL Server Analysis Services development tools to construct a customer classification data mining model, performed specified drilling based on clustering algorithms, and obtained an initial 10 life insurance customer classification groups.
  • Analyzed the characteristics of various customer groups through visual charts and conducted cross-comparisons. Based on similarity in classification indicators, reclassified 10 customer groups into 1 general customer group and 2 VIP target groups, which provided data support and decision-making basis for customer relationship management and targeted marketing for the life insurance company.

05/2025–08/2025

Analysis of Financial Transaction Fraud Detection Based on Machine Learning Algorithms

Supervisor: Yi Ke

Purpose

Identify indicators strongly correlated with fraudulent behaviors and improve financial transaction fraud detection.

View my contribution

My contribution

  • Conducted research on 50,000 pieces of real financial transaction data from January 1, 2023 to December 31, 2023.
  • Employed Python programming language and built models using Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost algorithms to identify indicators strongly correlated with fraudulent behaviors, optimizing and improving evaluation metrics such as precision and accuracy of fraud detection.
  • Achieved a high fraud detection rate with the final model, and the conclusions have certain reference significance for subsequent financial transaction fraud detection.

PUBLICATION: Proceedings of the 2025 3rd International Conference on Data Science, Advanced Algorithms, and Intelligent Computing (DAI 2025). Shanghai, China, 2025. (Paper ID: DAI25138)
